## Q1 - AI, ML, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Agents
### A - Answer
AI (Artificial Intelligence): The broad field of creating machines that can perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence.
  ex: A chess-playing computer
ML (Machine Learning): A subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions.
  ex: Email spam detection
Deep Learning: A subset of ML that uses multi-layered neural networks to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.
  ex:AI: Face recognition
Generative AI: AI that can create new content such as text, images, audio, video, or code.
  ex: ChatGPT generating an essay or an image model generating a picture
AI Agents: AI systems that can understand a goal, plan steps, use tools, and take actions to complete tasks.
  ex: An AI that searches information, plans a trip, and performs actions using connected tools
